Per unit multiplies the entered cost by the work item’s quantity. If the pricing basis changes to “per item,” the cost option follows it.

Flat total uses the entered cost once. A cost measured with a different quantity—such as labor hours on a square-foot quote—would need an optional advanced entry later.
