ServiceBuddy product concept

A composable Trash system

Status answers where a record is in the business. Trash answers whether it should still appear. Those should be separate everywhere.

Recommended standard

One-click soft delete with Undo, a shared 30-day Trash area, and confirmation only for permanent deletion or records with important dependencies.

Three functionally different systems

These change the record lifecycle and recovery model, not merely the confirmation screen.

Shared lifecycle
Active→Trashed→ 30 days →Purged

Store deletedAt, deletedBy, and an optional reason on every trashable resource. Normal queries automatically exclude trashed records.

Linked records are preserved by default. Trashing a lead never silently deletes its contact, quote, files, or project.
